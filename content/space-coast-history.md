## A history bigger than rockets

The Space Coast name is inseparable from Cape Canaveral, Kennedy Space Center, and the people who sent spacecraft beyond Earth. But Brevard County history also includes Indigenous communities, citrus growers, fishing families, railroad towns, military installations, surfers, teachers, small businesses, and neighborhoods transformed almost overnight by the space program.

This page is a starting point for those stories.

These official resources preserve places and dates. Oral histories add the voices.

## The human side of the space program

Space Coast families often possess the details that do not appear in mission summaries: the shift schedules, traffic jams, launch-day rituals, handwritten calculations, family separations, workplace friendships, and the strange normality of watching history rise over the river.

Read our [questions to ask someone who worked at NASA or Kennedy Space Center](/blog/questions-to-ask-nasa-kennedy-space-center-worker/) or meet [JoAnn Morgan, the only woman in the Apollo 11 firing room](/blog/joann-morgan-apollo-11-firing-room/).

## People with Space Coast beginnings

Local history also travels through individual lives. Explore our collection of [famous people from Florida's Space Coast](/blog/famous-people-from-floridas-space-coast/), including astronaut Winston Scott, surfer Kelly Slater, musician Jim Morrison, comedian Darrell Hammond, and others whose early years intersected with Brevard County.

Fame is not the requirement. The machinist, teacher, nurse, motel owner, military spouse, citrus worker, police officer, boat captain, and launch technician all experienced versions of the Space Coast that are disappearing from living memory.
