Interview Day Checklist

Before

  • Choose one theme, not an entire lifetime.
  • Charge the phone or recorder.
  • Bring a second recording device if possible.
  • Find a quiet room with soft furniture.
  • Ask permission to record.

Bring

  • Five photographs or objects
  • Water and a light snack
  • Names and dates you already know
  • Paper for follow-up notes
  • Patience for pauses

During

  • Ask one question at a time.
  • Wait three seconds longer than feels natural.
  • Ask, “What happened next?”
  • Do not correct the story mid-memory.
  • Stop before everyone is exhausted.

After

  • Back up the recording twice.
  • Write down spellings and open questions.
  • Name the file with the person and date.
  • Send a thank-you.
  • Choose the next small story.

Questions for an Old Photograph

  1. Who took this photograph, and why that day?
  2. What happened five minutes before it?
  3. What do you remember about the room or place?
  4. What could you hear nearby?
  5. What were you wearing, and how did you feel in it?
  6. Who is missing from the photograph?
  7. What was happening in the family then that the picture does not show?
  8. Which person here changed the most afterward?
  9. What happened later that day?
  10. If this photograph could speak, what would it correct?
  11. Who should have this picture someday?
  12. What is the story you never want the caption to lose?
